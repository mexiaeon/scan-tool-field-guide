Our approach

Useful guidance, without the sales noise.

Scan Tool Field Guide is an independent informational project focused on automotive diagnostic equipment. Our aim is to help readers understand scan-tool capabilities, vehicle compatibility, update policies and practical trade-offs before they buy.

How we research

  • Manufacturer-first sourcing. We prioritize product pages, manuals, coverage databases and support documentation from the companies that make the tools.
  • Clear testing labels. A specification-based comparison is not a hands-on test. We will say when we have not personally used a product.
  • Vehicle-specific caveats. A feature may work on one vehicle and not another. We avoid presenting marketing feature counts as universal compatibility guarantees.
  • Ownership costs. We consider update periods, subscriptions, optional adapters, warranty and regional restrictions where information is available.
  • Corrections matter. Product features and software policies change. Pages should show when the information was last checked and link to primary sources.

What we do not claim

We do not claim to be a repair shop, a vehicle manufacturer, an authorized distributor or a certification body. Unless a page explicitly says otherwise, our product information is desk research—not independent laboratory testing.

Safety

Diagnostic tools do not replace service information, training or safe repair procedures. Work involving airbags, brakes, steering, high-voltage components or vehicle programming may require qualified professionals.
