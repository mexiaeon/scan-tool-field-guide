Autel vs. XTOOL vs. TOPDON: Which Diagnostic Scanner Should You Choose?

Three mid-level diagnostic tablets, three different trade-offs. Compare their published hardware, active-test capabilities, service functions, connectivity and update policies before deciding which one fits your work.

Quick answer

There is no universal winner. The Autel MaxiCOM MK900-BT has the strongest published tablet hardware of these three on the specifications reviewed, with 4 GB RAM, 64 GB storage and an 8-inch display. The XTOOL D7S advertises the broadest service-function count (39+) and selected ECU coding capabilities, but the standard specification lists a wired vehicle connection and three years of free updates. The TOPDON ArtiDiag900 Lite combines an 8-inch display, wireless VCI and eight listed service functions; TOPDON USA currently advertises free lifetime updates for eligible US owners.

Choose based on the exact vehicle functions you need, not the biggest number on a product page.

1. Autel MaxiCOM MK900-BT

The Autel stands out for its tablet specifications: Android 11, a quad-core 1.8 GHz processor, 4 GB RAM, 64 GB storage and an 8-inch 1280 × 800 screen. Autel lists a wireless MaxiVCI V150 connection and protocols including CAN FD and DoIP. Its official product description lists broad OE-level system coverage and service functions such as oil reset, EPB, SAS, BMS and DPF.

Best reason to shortlist it: You value a larger, higher-spec tablet, wireless vehicle communication and a broad diagnostic platform.

Check before buying: The official store listing includes one year of free updates and one year of warranty. Ask about renewal pricing and confirm each active test and service operation for your vehicle.

2. XTOOL D7S

The standard D7S specification lists Android 10, a 7-inch 1024 × 600 screen, 2 GB RAM, 64 GB storage and a wired vehicle connection. XTOOL advertises full bidirectional control, 39+ special functions, selected VW/Audi/Skoda ECU coding, graphing for up to eight data streams and CAN FD / DoIP support. Its standard specification page lists three years of free upgrades.

Best reason to shortlist it: You want a large advertised service-function menu and the specific coding or diagnostic functions XTOOL lists for supported vehicles.

Check before buying: The 39+ count is not a promise that every function works on every vehicle. Separate standard D7S features from D7S V2.0 features, especially FCA AutoAuth, PMI and online coding. Check whether VAG guided functions are currently available in your region.

Which one should you buy?

Choose Autel if…

  • You prioritize the higher published RAM and tablet specifications.
  • You want an 8-inch screen and wireless VCI.
  • The Autel service and system coverage matches your vehicle and diagnostic jobs.

Choose XTOOL if…

  • You need one or more of its advertised 39+ special functions.
  • You work on vehicles for which its selected ECU coding functions are confirmed.
  • You are comfortable with the standard D7S wired connection and its three-year update term.

Choose TOPDON if…

  • You want an 8-inch tablet with wireless VCI.
  • The eight listed service functions are sufficient for your work.
  • You qualify for TOPDON USA's lifetime-update policy and value that ownership term.

Five checks before you spend money

  1. Confirm exact vehicle coverage. Give the manufacturer your year, make, model, engine and trim.
  2. Name the exact operation. Ask whether the scanner can perform the specific active test, reset, adaptation or coding job—not simply whether it supports the brand.
  3. Confirm the hardware version. This matters especially for XTOOL D7S vs. D7S V2.0.
  4. Calculate update costs. Check the included period, renewal price, what remains usable after expiry and regional policy.
  5. Check seller terms. Verify stock, warranty, return policy, included VCI and regional support.

Our bottom line

On published hardware alone, Autel has the strongest tablet specification in this group. XTOOL advertises the most service functions and selected coding capabilities, while TOPDON's wireless setup and US lifetime-update policy are attractive if its more limited listed service menu covers your needs. These are differences in published specifications—not proof of real-world speed, reliability or diagnostic accuracy.

The right choice is the scanner that demonstrably supports the systems and procedures you need on your own vehicles, with acceptable update and warranty costs. If a seller cannot confirm a critical function for your exact vehicle, do not buy based on a generic feature count.
