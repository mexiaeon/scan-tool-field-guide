What does the ArtiDiag900 Lite offer?

  • Full-system diagnostics: TOPDON advertises diagnostic access across 60+ vehicle brands, including supported engine, transmission, ABS, airbag and other electronic systems.
  • Bidirectional control: The tool can send supported commands to vehicle components and modules. TOPDON gives examples such as fuel injectors, coolant pumps, windows and windshield wipers.
  • Eight service functions: The US product page lists oil reset, brake reset, steering-angle-sensor reset, battery-management reset, ABS bleeding, ETS reset, DPF reset and airbag reset.
  • Wireless VCI: A Bluetooth-connected vehicle interface lets the tablet communicate without remaining physically tethered to the OBD connector.
  • Repair Data Library: TOPDON promotes repair information and DTC guidance intended to help users investigate fault codes and plan repairs.
  • Standard OBD2 diagnostics: The product supports the familiar OBD2 functions for reading and clearing codes and viewing diagnostic information. The complete list of supported modes should be checked in the manual and for the vehicle.

Bidirectional control: what it means

Bidirectional control allows a compatible scan tool to send a command to a vehicle module during a diagnostic procedure. For example, a technician may command a supported window, wiper, coolant pump or fuel injector function and observe the response. This can help narrow down a fault when used alongside wiring diagrams, measurements and normal diagnostic procedures.

It is not a guarantee that every actuator can be commanded on every car. The vehicle’s year, make, model, trim, control module and software determine which tests are available. A tool may support bidirectional control generally while offering only a subset of tests for a particular vehicle.

Eight service functions: what is included?

TOPDON’s current US product specification lists these eight functions:

  • Oil reset: Reset the maintenance reminder after the appropriate service.
  • Brake reset: Support selected electronic parking-brake service procedures.
  • SAS reset: Steering angle sensor reset or calibration where supported.
  • BMS reset: Battery-management registration or reset functions on supported vehicles.
  • ABS bleeding: Guided or commanded ABS hydraulic bleeding procedures where supported.
  • ETS reset: Electronic throttle system reset or adaptation where supported.
  • DPF reset: Diesel particulate filter service or regeneration-related functions where supported.
  • Airbag reset: SRS-related reset capability where supported; this does not replace proper diagnosis or repair of a safety-system fault.

Do not assume every function works on every supported make. Ask TOPDON to confirm the exact operation for your vehicle before purchase. Follow the vehicle service manual and safety procedures, especially for brake, airbag and emissions systems.

Vehicle coverage: 60+ brands is not universal coverage

TOPDON advertises coverage for more than 60 vehicle brands, and its current US feature table lists 65+. Brand count is a broad indicator, not a guarantee that every module or special function is supported across every model year. TOPDON’s page specifically notes that its compatibility for Chrysler, Dodge, Jeep and Fiat vehicles is currently limited to 2010–2014 models, with compatibility expanding through updates.

Before buying, use TOPDON’s vehicle coverage resource or contact support with the exact year, make, model, engine and the function you need. Ask separately about all-system scans, active tests and each service function; they can have different coverage.

Software updates and warranty: an important 2026 change

Older product profiles and manuals may refer to two years of free updates. TOPDON USA announced on August 25, 2026 that the ArtiDiag900 Lite would receive free lifetime software updates, effective August 18, 2026, for current and future owners. The current US product page also states free lifetime updates and a one-year warranty.

This is a US policy announcement. If you buy from a different regional store or marketplace seller, confirm that the lifetime-update policy applies to your region, serial number and exact unit. Also confirm the warranty provider and return terms.

Potential advantages and trade-offs

What looks strong on paper

  • Large 8-inch, 1280 × 800 display.
  • Wireless Bluetooth VCI connection for more flexible use around the vehicle.
  • Full-system diagnostics and bidirectional control are listed by TOPDON.
  • 10,000 mAh battery and 32 GB internal storage.
  • Eight common service functions cover several routine maintenance procedures.
  • TOPDON USA's lifetime-update policy is a meaningful ownership-cost advantage for eligible US buyers.

What to consider before buying

  • Only eight listed service functions: Some competing scanners advertise substantially longer reset-function lists. Compare the exact functions you need, not just the headline count.
  • 2 GB RAM / 32 GB storage: The hardware is adequate for its intended scanner role but is not as generously specified as some newer tablets.
  • Vehicle-specific limitations: Brand coverage does not mean every active test or service function works on every model.
  • Regional update terms: The lifetime-update announcement is from TOPDON USA; verify terms outside the US.
  • Not a universal programming tool: Do not assume the Lite model provides unrestricted ECU programming, key programming or coding unless the exact function is explicitly listed for your vehicle.

Who should consider it?

Worth shortlisting: Advanced DIY owners and independent technicians who value an 8-inch display, wireless VCI, full-system diagnostics, supported active tests and a focused set of eight maintenance functions.

Probably more tool than necessary: Drivers who only want to read and clear basic check-engine codes or check emissions readiness. A simpler OBD2 reader may be less expensive.

Compare another scanner: If you need a much wider menu of service resets, selected ECU coding or a different update arrangement, compare the XTOOL D7S. If you want a wireless tablet with a different hardware and feature balance, compare the Autel MK900-BT.
