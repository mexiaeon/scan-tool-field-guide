Quick verdict

The Autel MaxiCOM MK900-BT is a wireless, tablet-style diagnostic tool aimed at advanced DIY users, mobile technicians and independent workshops that need more than basic engine-code reading. Bidirectional control: what it means in practice

Bidirectional control lets a compatible scan tool send a command to a vehicle module during a diagnostic procedure. A technician might use a supported active test to command an actuator and observe whether it responds. This can help narrow a fault, but it does not automatically identify the cause or prove a component is defective.

Before buying, check whether the specific active test you need is supported for your exact year, make, model, engine and module. “Bidirectional” is not a promise that every actuator or system can be commanded on every vehicle.

Service functions and TPMS distinction

Autel lists a range of service and maintenance functions for the MK900-BT. Examples include oil reset, EPB, SAS, BMS and DPF-related service operations. The availability and procedure can differ by vehicle.

One important model distinction: Autel’s comparison table lists basic TPMS capability for the MK900-BT, while the MK900-TS is the variant positioned for more comprehensive TPMS diagnostics and sensor activation/programming. If advanced TPMS work is central to your business, compare the TS model and verify the exact TPMS functions before choosing.

Potential advantages and trade-offs

What looks strong on paper

  • Wireless VCI connection rather than a permanently tethered diagnostic cable.
  • 8-inch display with 4 GB RAM and 64 GB storage.
  • All-systems diagnostic access and active-test capability, subject to vehicle coverage.
  • CAN FD and DoIP support for vehicles that use those protocols.
  • Pre- and post-scan reporting features that may suit workshop documentation.

What to think about before purchasing

  • Coverage is not universal: Verify each required module and function against Autel’s coverage information.
  • Not a full ECU programming platform: Do not assume active tests or service functions mean unrestricted ECU coding or programming.
  • TPMS is not the main strength of this variant: The MK900-TS offers more extensive TPMS features according to Autel’s comparison.
  • Software terms matter: Confirm the included update period, renewal price, regional software and any online/security-gateway requirements with the seller.
  • Bundle contents can vary: Check whether the listing includes only the tablet and VCI or optional accessories such as a battery tester or videoscope.

Who should consider it?

Worth shortlisting: Experienced DIY users, mobile mechanics and independent shops that want wireless all-systems diagnostics, active tests and a broad set of service functions—and have confirmed the features they need for their customer vehicles.

Probably more tool than necessary: Drivers who only want to read a check-engine light, clear basic emissions codes or check readiness monitors. A simpler OBD2 reader may be more economical.

Compare another model: If advanced TPMS sensor work is essential, compare the MK900-TS. If you do not need wireless communication, compare the wired MK900 and its current price and included features.
